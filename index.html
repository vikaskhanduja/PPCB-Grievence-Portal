    Why Use This Portal

    A comprehensive Environemnt-related grievance management platform for the citizens of Punjab

    Timely resolution

    Every complaint tracked against defined timelines. Auto-escalation on overdue cases.

    Real-Time Alerts

    Automatic SMS and email alerts to complainants and officers at every stage.

    Appeal Mechanism

    PPCB allows two-tier appeal: First to the Senior Environmental Engineer followed by the Member Secretary within 45 and 90 days of disposal, respectively.

    Audit Trail

    Complete digital record of every action. Transparent, accountable, tamper-proof.
