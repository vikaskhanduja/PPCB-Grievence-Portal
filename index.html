Why Use This Portal

A fully SOP-compliant grievance management platform for Punjab's citizens

Multi-Channel Intake

Portal, Email, WhatsApp, Letter, Social Media — all routed into one unified system.

SOP-Compliant TAT

Every complaint tracked against defined timelines. Auto-escalation on overdue cases.

Role-Based Workflow

GRM Cell → Ex-EN → RO → SDO/ADO. Clear responsibilities, no manual paperwork.

Real-Time Alerts

Appeal Mechanism

Two-level appeal: First to CEE, Second to Member Secretary. Full history available.

Audit Trail

10-Step Workflow

Complaint Resolution Journey

From submission to final closure — SOP-compliant at every step

1
Multi-Channel Intake

Portal, Email, Letter, WhatsApp, Social Media into one system.

2
Registration & OTP

Unique complaint number generated; auto-acknowledgement via SMS & Email.

3
Scrutiny by Ex-EN

Review jurisdiction, completeness, and allocate to appropriate level.

4
RO Allocation

Regional Officer receives case digitally with SOP timelines.

5
Field Inspection

SDO/ADO visits site, collects evidence, uploads inspection report.

6
Regulatory Action

Advisory, Notice, or Order issued. Compliance monitored via follow-up.

7
TAT Monitoring

Auto-escalation if SOP timelines are breached.

8
ATR & Disposal

Action Taken Report uploaded; complaint marked disposed.

9
Feedback & Appeal

Two-level appeal mechanism available if unsatisfied.

10
Final Closure

Closed with complete audit trail retained.
